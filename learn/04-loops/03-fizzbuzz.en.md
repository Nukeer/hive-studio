# FizzBuzz

The classic. Write `fizzBuzz`, which counts from 1 to `n` on one line of text, separated by spaces — except that every multiple of 3 is `Fizz`, every multiple of 5 is `Buzz`, and every multiple of both is `FizzBuzz`.

```
fizzBuzz(5)   →  "1 2 Fizz 4 Buzz"
fizzBuzz(15)  →  "1 2 Fizz 4 Buzz Fizz 7 8 Fizz Buzz 11 Fizz 13 14 FizzBuzz"
fizzBuzz(0)   →  ""
```

Strings join with `+`: `"bee" + "s"` is `"bees"`.

## Hint

Test for 15 before 3 and 5. Put a space *before* every word but the first, and the line never starts or ends with one.
